WorkDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

WorkDesk is a workforce attendance application operated by Binimise Labs. This policy explains what the app collects, why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

WorkDesk is used by organisations to record staff attendance. If your employer gave you this app, your employer decides what is recorded about you and for how long. Binimise Labs processes that information on their instructions.

Information we collect

WhatWhyWhen
Name, work mobile number or email, company name, job role To create your account and identify you to your organisation When you sign up or are added by your employer
Precise location (GPS) To confirm you are at your assigned work site when you punch in or out Only at the moment you punch. Not collected in the background.
Face image To verify that the person punching is the person enrolled When you enrol your face, and at each face punch
Attendance records Punch times, work site, hours, present or absent status Each time you punch
Device and app information Device model, OS version, app version and crash diagnostics, to keep the app working While you use the app
Notification token To deliver announcements from your organisation If you allow notifications
We do not collect your contacts, your photo library, your messages, your call history, or your location at any time other than when you punch. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

  • Location — asked for at your first punch, with an explanation. Used only to check your position against the geofence of your assigned site at that moment.
  • Camera — asked for when you enrol your face or take a face punch. Used only to capture that image.
  • Notifications — asked for so your organisation can reach you with announcements.

You may decline any permission. The app will tell you which feature becomes unavailable. You can change or revoke a permission at any time in your device settings; WorkDesk re-checks each permission immediately before it uses the capability.

Face data

Your face image is used for one purpose: to verify your identity at an attendance punch. It is not used to identify you anywhere else, is not shared with third parties for their own purposes, and is not used to train any model. It is stored on servers operated by Binimise Labs for your employer, and is deleted when your employer deletes your staff record or when you ask us to delete your account.

How information is shared

  • Your employer sees your attendance records, your work site and your profile. That is the purpose of the app.
  • Service providers that host our servers, send one-time codes and deliver push notifications process data on our behalf under contract, and may not use it for anything else.
  • Legal — we disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your work mobile or email, and an optional PIN stored on your device. Access tokens, one-time codes and PINs are never written to application logs.

How long we keep it

Attendance records are kept for as long as your employer's retention policy requires, which is usually set by local employment and payroll law. Account details and face data are deleted within 30 days of your staff record being removed or your account being deleted.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you. Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days. If your employer controls the data, we will pass your request to them and tell you we have done so.

Children

WorkDesk is a workplace tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
