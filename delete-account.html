Delete your WorkDesk account

WorkDesk, by Binimise Labs · Android package com.binimise.staff.services

This page explains how to ask us to delete your WorkDesk account and the data held against it. You do not need to be signed in to make the request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to privacy@binimise.com with the subject WorkDesk account deletion request.
  2. Include the mobile number or email address you use to sign in to WorkDesk, and the company name your account belongs to.
  3. Say whether you want your whole account deleted, or only specific data such as your enrolled face image.
  4. We reply to confirm the request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days.
If you still work for the organisation that added you to WorkDesk, tell your administrator as well. They control the attendance records kept about you, and employment or payroll law may require them to retain some of those records even after your account is removed.

What is deleted

DataWhat happensWhen
Your profile — name, mobile number, email, job role DeletedWithin 30 days of the request
Your enrolled face image and face template DeletedWithin 30 days of the request
Your sign-in session and PIN Deleted immediately, signing you out of every deviceOn deletion
Notification tokenDeletedOn deletion
Attendance records — punch times, work site, hours Kept by your employer for as long as their retention policy and local employment or payroll law require, then deleted. Ask us to delete only these and we will pass the request to your employer and tell you we have done so. Per employer policy
BackupsOverwritten on the normal backup rotationWithin 90 days

Deleting only some data

You can ask us to delete your face enrolment while keeping your account. Say so in the email and you will be able to keep using WorkDesk, enrolling your face again later if your organisation requires a face punch.
